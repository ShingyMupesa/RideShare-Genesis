About / Intro (longer description):
RideShare Genesis is a Kenyan ride-sharing platform for matatu, PSV and taxi drivers and the people who ride with them. Drivers post their route, time and fare, and riders going the same way book a seat ahead. Drivers keep 90% of every fare (95% on Pro Driver). Fares are shown up front, drivers can earn a verified badge, and every trip has one-tap SOS. Free to join, in English and Kiswahili.
