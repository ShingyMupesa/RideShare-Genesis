Mission

RideShare Genesis is a human-centred, technology-enabled mobility platform designed to help people identify and fulfil real mobility needs through shared journeys, intelligent assistance, trust infrastructure, and measurable impact.

The problem

Ride-matching is a black box everywhere — riders can't see why they were paired with a given driver, and drivers face opaque commission structures they can't audit. In markets where ridesharing is still informal — Nairobi's matatu culture included — trust has to be earned trip by trip, not assumed.

The solution

Every match explains itself.

  • Decision DNA — a live, weighted score across six factors (route fit, timing, price, preferences, reliability, and environmental fit) shown to the rider, not hidden — and personally retunable.
  • Verified Driver review — licence, vehicle registration, and insurance checked before a driver can post, honestly labelled for what it does and doesn't yet certify.
  • Mobile-money-native — M-Pesa and card payments live from day one, a shillings-first product, not a card-first one retrofitted for Kenya.
Traction

V1 is fully built and live in production at ridesharegenesis.app — real M-Pesa & card payments, a working matching engine, a Safety Centre, driver verification. Deliberately pre-launch on user acquisition: commission plumbing is live end-to-end at 0% while we seed the first cohort. This round funds the go-to-market push that hasn't started yet.
