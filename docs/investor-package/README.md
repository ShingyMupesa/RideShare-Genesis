# RideShare Genesis — Investor & Compliance Package

Compiled for easy access. Everything here reflects the platform as actually
built in this repository at the time of compilation — nothing in this folder
is aspirational or written independently of the product.

## Artifacts

- [`artifacts/pitch-deck.html`](artifacts/pitch-deck.html) — a standalone
  copy of the live pitch deck. Open it directly in a browser.
- Live, always-current version:
  https://claude.ai/code/artifact/483c8e4d-d5b8-44e0-a01e-cc3f7b57a9eb

## Documents

| File | What it is | Status |
|------|------------|--------|
| `documents/RideShare-Genesis-Pitch-Deck.pdf` | A portable snapshot of the pitch deck, for sending directly | Ready to share |
| `documents/RideShare-Genesis-Blueprint.docx` | Product & technical blueprint — architecture, data model, matching engine, roadmap | Ready to share |
| `documents/RideShare-Genesis-Compliance-Overview.docx` | Data protection, payments, and regulatory posture | **Draft — needs legal review** |
| `documents/RideShare-Genesis-Terms-and-Conditions.docx` | Draft user-facing Terms & Conditions | **Draft — needs legal review** |

The two documents marked "draft — needs legal review" were generated from
the platform's actual features by Claude, not written or reviewed by a
lawyer. They must be reviewed and approved by qualified counsel, licensed in
the relevant jurisdiction(s), before being published, relied upon, or shown
to a regulator as a compliance representation. See each document's opening
banner for the full disclaimer.

The in-app equivalent of the Terms & Conditions lives at `/terms` in the
product itself and is what new users must tick a checkbox to accept before
registration — kept in sync in substance with the drafted document here, but
maintained separately since it's part of the live product.

## Regenerating this package

- The pitch deck HTML/PDF are snapshots of the published Claude Artifact —
  re-save from the artifact when the deck changes.
- The three `.docx` documents were generated by a docx-js script; regenerate
  by editing the source content and re-running the generator rather than
  hand-editing the `.docx` files, so this README and the documents don't
  drift out of sync with each other.
